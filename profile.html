India is one of the oldest civilization in the world with a kaleidoscopic variety and rich cultural heritage.In the world,Indian stands apart from the rest of asia, marked off as it is by mountains and the sea, it stretches southwards and at the tropic of cancer, tapers off into the Indian ocean between the bay of Bengal on the east and the Arabian Sea on the west.

Lying entirely in the northern hemisphere, the mainland extends between latitudes 8 4' and 37 6' north logitudes 68 7' and 97 25' east and measures about 3,214 km from north to south between the extreme latitudes and about 2,933 km from east to west between the extreme longitudes. It has a land frontier of about 15,200 km. The total length of the coastline of the mainland, Lashadweep Island and Andaman & Nicobar Islands is 7,516.6 km.
